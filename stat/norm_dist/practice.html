Turn a real value (X) into a Z-score using Z = (X − μ) ÷ σ

Type 1 — Finding the Z-Score

Question 1

The average height of students in a school is 160 cm, and the standard deviation is 10 cm. If Sara is 175 cm tall, what is her Z-score?

See formula
Z = (X − μ) ÷ σ

X = the value in the question, μ = mean, σ = standard deviation. Z tells you how many σ the value is above (+) or below (−) the mean.

Type 6 — Confidence Intervals (Z-Interval)

Question 1

A researcher picks 50 students and finds their average study time is 3.2 hours per day. The population standard deviation is 0.8 hours. Build a 95% confidence interval for the true average study time of all students.

See formula
CI = X̄ ± Z* · (σ ÷ √n)

Z* = 1.645 (90%) · 1.96 (95%) · 2.576 (99%). The part after ± is the margin of error, E.

Type 1 — Finding the Z-Score

Turn a real value (X) into a Z-score using Z = (X − μ) ÷ σ

Type 1 · Worked solution

Question 1

The average height of students in a school is 160 cm, and the standard deviation is 10 cm. If Sara is 175 cm tall, what is her Z-score?

Solution

Z = (X − μ) ÷ σ = (175 − 160) ÷ 10 = 15 ÷ 10 = 1.50. Sara is 1.5 standard deviations above the mean.

Type 1 · Worked solution

Question 2

A factory makes bags of chips. The average weight is 200 grams with a standard deviation of 5 grams. One bag weighs 190 grams. What is the Z-score for this bag?

Solution

Z = (190 − 200) ÷ 5 = −10 ÷ 5 = −2.00. The bag is 2 standard deviations below the mean.

Type 1 · Worked solution

Question 3

Scores on a math test follow a normal distribution with a mean of 70 and standard deviation of 8. Tom scored 86. Find Tom's Z-score.

Solution

Z = (86 − 70) ÷ 8 = 16 ÷ 8 = 2.00. Tom is 2 standard deviations above the mean.

Type 1 · Worked solution

Question 4

A delivery company says packages arrive in an average of 5 days with a standard deviation of 1.5 days. One package arrived in 3 days. What is the Z-score?

Solution

Z = (3 − 5) ÷ 1.5 = −2 ÷ 1.5 = −1.33 (approximately). The package arrived 1.33 standard deviations faster than average.

Type 1 · Worked solution

Question 5

Monthly sales at a store average $50,000 with a standard deviation of $8,000. Last month, the store sold $62,000. Calculate the Z-score for last month's sales.

Solution

Z = (62,000 − 50,000) ÷ 8,000 = 12,000 ÷ 8,000 = 1.50. Sales were 1.5 standard deviations above the mean.

Question 1

Scores on a business exam are normally distributed with mean 72 and standard deviation 9. A student needs to be in the top 10% to win a prize. What is the minimum score they need?

Solution

Top 10% means 90% of scores are below it. Left area = 0.90.
From the Z-table, Z ≈ 1.28.
X = μ + Z · σ = 72 + (1.28 × 9) = 83.52.
Minimum score needed: about 83.5.

X = μ + Z · σ = 162 + (−0.84 × 6) = 156.96.
The shortest 20% are below about 157.0 cm.

Type 6 · Solutions

Type 6 — Confidence Intervals (Z-Interval)

CI = X̄ ± Z* · (σ ÷ √n)  |  Z* = 1.645 (90%), 1.96 (95%), 2.576 (99%)

Type 6 · Worked solution

Question 1

A researcher picks 50 students and finds their average study time is 3.2 hours per day. The population standard deviation is 0.8 hours. Build a 95% confidence interval for the true average study time of all students.

Solution

E = Z* · (σ ÷ √n) = 1.96 × (0.8 ÷ √50) = 1.96 × 0.1131 = 0.2217.
CI = 3.2 ± 0.2217 = (2.98, 3.42) hours.

Type 6 · Worked solution

Question 2

A store manager surveys 36 customers and finds the average purchase amount is $45. The known standard deviation is $12. Construct a 90% confidence interval for the true average purchase amount.

Solution

E = 1.645 × (12 ÷ √36) = 1.645 × 2 = 3.29.
CI = 45 ± 3.29 = ($41.71, $48.29).

Type 6 · Worked solution

Question 3

A factory checks 100 bolts and finds the average diameter is 10.3 mm. The population standard deviation is 0.5 mm. Find the 99% confidence interval for the true average diameter of all bolts.

Solution

E = 2.576 × (0.5 ÷ √100) = 2.576 × 0.05 = 0.1288.
CI = 10.3 ± 0.1288 = (10.17, 10.43) mm.

Type 6 · Worked solution

Question 4

A hospital records the recovery time of 64 patients. The sample average is 7.5 days and the population standard deviation is 2 days. Construct a 95% confidence interval for the true mean recovery time. Then write what the interval means in plain words.

Solution

E = 1.96 × (2 ÷ √64) = 1.96 × 0.25 = 0.49.
CI = 7.5 ± 0.49 = (7.01, 7.99) days.
Meaning: we are 95% confident that the true average recovery time of all patients is between 7.01 and 7.99 days.

Type 6 · Worked solution

Question 5

A telecom company samples 81 customers. Average monthly bill = $72, population standard deviation = $9. (a) Build a 90% confidence interval. (b) Build a 99% confidence interval. (c) Which interval is wider, and why?

Solution
