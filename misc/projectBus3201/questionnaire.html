Questionnaire (QTR) | BUS3201 Research Methodology | Fall 2026

Opening text for the Google Form

Hello! We are BBA students at the University of Liberal Arts Bangladesh (ULAB). We are doing a class research study on how university students in Dhaka choose where to shop: on digital platforms or in offline shops. The survey takes about 5 minutes. We do not ask for your name. Your answers are anonymous and are used only for this class study. You can stop at any time. Thank you for your help.

Digital platforms = Daraz, Facebook shops, brand websites and shopping apps. Offline shops = shops, shopping malls and markets that you visit in person.

Note for students: the small codes in brackets, such as [DPI1], are the column names in your Excel dataset. Do not type the codes in your Google Form. For Q10 to Q29, use “Linear scale” from 1 (Strongly disagree) to 5 (Strongly agree).

Part A: About you

Q1. Do you agree to take part in this survey? (Multiple choice, required)

  • Yes, I agree (go on)
  • No, I do not agree (end the survey)

Q2. Are you now a student at a university in Dhaka? (Multiple choice, required)

  • Yes (go on)
  • No (end the survey)

Q3. What is your age group? [AgeGroup] (Multiple choice)

  • 18 to 20
  • 21 to 23
  • 24 or above

Q4. What is your gender? [Gender] (Multiple choice)

  • Male
  • Female
  • Prefer not to say

Q5. What type of university do you study in? [UniType] (Multiple choice)

  • Public
  • Private

Q6. What is your family’s total monthly income (in Taka)? [Income] (Multiple choice)

  • Below 30,000
  • 30,000 to 60,000
  • 60,001 to 100,000
  • Above 100,000

Q7. How many times do you shop in a usual month (online or offline)? [ShopFreq] (Multiple choice)

  • Once or less
  • 2 to 3 times
  • 4 times or more

Q8. Did you buy anything from a digital platform in the past 30 days? [BoughtOnline30] (Multiple choice)

  • Yes
  • No

Q9. About how much money did you spend on digital platforms in the past 30 days? Write the amount in Taka, numbers only. Write 0 if you spent nothing. [Spend30] (Short answer, number only. In Google Forms: Response validation > Number)

Rating scale for Q10 to Q29

1 = Strongly disagree, 2 = Disagree, 3 = Neutral, 4 = Agree, 5 = Strongly agree (Linear scale 1 to 5)

Part B: Buying on digital platforms

  • Q10. I plan to buy products on digital platforms in the near future. [DPI1]
  • Q11. If I had a choice, I would use digital platforms as my main way of shopping. [DPI2]
  • Q12. I expect to shop on digital platforms more often in the next few months. [DPI3]
  • Q13. I would suggest digital platforms to people I know as a good place to shop. [DPI4]

Part C: Buying in offline shops

  • Q14. I plan to buy products from offline shops in the near future. [OPI1]
  • Q15. If I had a choice, I would use offline shops as my main way of shopping. [OPI2]
  • Q16. I expect to visit offline shops more often in the next few months. [OPI3]
  • Q17. I would suggest offline shops to people I know as a good place to shop. [OPI4]

Part D: Price value of digital platforms

  • Q18. Prices on digital platforms are affordable for me. [PV1]
  • Q19. Discounts and deals on digital platforms give me good value for money. [PV2]
  • Q20. On digital platforms, it is easy to compare the prices of different sellers. [PV3]
  • Q21. When I add all costs (such as delivery charges), buying on digital platforms is cheaper for me than buying in offline shops. [PV4]

Part E: Convenience of digital platforms

  • Q22. Digital platforms let me shop at any time and from any place. [CV1]
  • Q23. Finding products on digital platforms takes less time and effort than going to a shop. [CV2]
  • Q24. Completing a purchase on a digital platform is quick and easy. [CV3]
  • Q25. Overall, shopping on digital platforms saves me time. [CV4]

Part F: Trust in digital platforms

  • Q26. I trust that products ordered from digital platforms will match their description and pictures. [TR1]
  • Q27. I believe the payment systems of digital platforms keep my money and information safe. [TR2]
  • Q28. I believe digital platforms will handle complaints and returns fairly. [TR3]
  • Q29. I feel safe when I buy from digital platforms. [TR4]

Part G: Your pocket money

Q30. How much pocket money do you get in a usual month? Write the amount in Taka, numbers only. Write 0 if you get none. [Allowance] (Short answer, number only. In Google Forms: Response validation > Number)

Closing text for the Google Form

Thank you for your time!
