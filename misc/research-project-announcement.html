What you will do

  1. Revised Research Proposal

    Update the proposal you submitted on 8 October. Your research knowledge is much better now, so use it. Your revised proposal must include:

    • a clear research problem
    • research objectives
    • research questions
    • hypotheses
    • variables
    • sampling plan
    • the statistical tests you will use

    You may change your topic. You may also choose the common topic that I have already given you.

  2. Questionnaire in Google Forms

    Make the questionnaire for your proposal in Google Forms. Share it with me as Editor (my usual email). I will edit it and approve it.

    After approval, collect a small pilot: 10 to 15 responses. This gives you real data-collection experience. Submit the response sheet.

  3. Data Analysis in Excel

    Each of you will get your own dataset, made for your questionnaire. No two students have the same data.

    The dataset is simulated (made by computer) for practice. It is not collected from real people.

    We will do the analysis together in 3 evening tutorial classes. After that, you finish the analysis and submit your Excel file.

  4. Final Report

    Write a report on the findings from your dataset. In the report, say clearly that the analysed data is simulated. Report your pilot (real) responses in a separate section. Submit both .docx and .pdf.

Get ready for Tutorial 1

Do this by Fri 20 Nov, 11:59 PM.

  1. Install AnyDesk on your PC or laptop.
  2. In Microsoft Excel, turn on the Analysis ToolPak:
    File > Options > Add-ins > Manage: Excel Add-ins > Go > tick Analysis ToolPak > OK
    YouTube video instructions for your MS Office version: 2016, 2019, 2021, Microsoft 365.
    Different version? Search YouTube for: how to enable Analysis ToolPak Excel plus your version (for example 2013 or Mac).
  3. Open Data > Data Analysis. Take a screenshot of the window that opens and post it in Classwork.

In every tutorial, join from two devices: your PC and your mobile phone, both in Google Classroom. I will connect to some students' PCs and share the screen with everyone. You will do the same steps on your own data at the same time.
