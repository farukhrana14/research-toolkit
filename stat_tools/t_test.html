T-Test Decision Tool

One-Sample, Independent Two-Sample, and Paired-Sample t-Tests

Important classroom notes

Choose the test based on what your data represents, not simply on sample size.

Welch: use for two independent groups when equal population variances are not assumed.

Pooled: use for two independent groups when equal population variances are reasonably assumed.

Paired: use when observations are naturally matched, such as before-and-after measurements on the same subjects.

Interpretation: a small p-value provides evidence against H₀; it does not by itself establish practical or causal significance.
